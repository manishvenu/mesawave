# Data requirements from streams

## WW3 Requirements
1. 10 m wind
2. Air temperature at the lowest model level (`Sa_tbot`)
3. Ocean Surface Currents

## WW3 Optional
1. Ocean Boundary Layer Depth
2. SST (`So_t`)
3. Sea Ice Ice Frac
4. Sea Ice Thickness
5. Sea Ice Floe Size

## MOM6 Requirements
1. Atm Bottom Winds
2. Air temperature and potential temperature at the lowest model level (`Sa_tbot`, `Sa_ptem`)
3. Specific Humidity
4. Air Density
5. Pressure
6. Reference Height
7. Radiation LongWave
8. Shortwave Fields
9. Precipitation
10. Sea Level pressure
11. Chemistry Fields for BGC
