## Ice fields the ocean might also want

If MOM6 runs with data ice instead of CICE, it takes these from the ice through the mediator:

1. Ice fraction (`Si_ifrac`)
2. Melt heat and meltwater (`Fioi_melth`, `Fioi_meltw`)
3. Salt flux (`Fioi_salt`)
4. Ice-ocean stress (`Fioi_taux`, `Fioi_tauy`)
5. Shortwave through the ice (`Fioi_swpen`, or `Fioi_swpen_ifrac_n` and `Si_ifrac_n` per ice category)
6. BGC only: black carbon and dust (`Fioi_bcphi`, `Fioi_bcpho`, `Fioi_flxdst`)

DICE's `ssmi` mode computes these ice-ocean fluxes; its `cplhist` mode exports none of them.
